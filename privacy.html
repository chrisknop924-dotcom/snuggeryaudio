Snuggery Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Snuggery ("the app," "we," "us") is an audiobook player for audio files you already own and keep on your phone. This policy explains what the app stores, what leaves your phone, and why.

The short version: Snuggery has no accounts and no servers of its own. We never receive your data. Your books, your listening spots, your bookmarks and your notes live on your phone. The only place any of it can go is your own Google Drive, if you choose to switch backup on, and the only people who can see your notes are the people you choose to send them to.

What Snuggery stores on your phone

All of this stays on your phone. Most of it is deleted when you uninstall the app; books you imported into the Music/Snuggery folder and any backup in your Google Drive are deliberately kept, so that you can get them back.

  • Which folders you told the app to look in, and a list of the audiobook files it found there (their names, running times and any title, author and cover art embedded in the files)
  • Your place in every book, your playback speed, your bookmarks, and which books you have finished
  • Notes you attach to a bookmark: text you type and voice notes you record
  • Your settings: skip lengths, sleep timer, Easy Mode, and similar
  • If you use Google Drive backup, the email address of the Google account you chose, so the app does not have to ask you again

What leaves your phone

  • Google Drive backup (optional). If you sign in and switch it on, the app saves a small file to your own Google Drive holding your listening spots, bookmarks, notes, finished books and, if you have recorded any, your voice notes. The "Book vault" option also copies the audiobook files themselves to your Drive. Everything goes to a folder called Snuggery in your Drive, and to nowhere else. The app asks Google only for permission to see files it created itself (the drive.file scope); it cannot read the rest of your Drive. You can sign out at any time, and you can delete the Snuggery folder from Drive whenever you like.
  • Cover art lookups (optional). When a book has no cover, the app can look one up online. To do that it sends the book's title and author to Google Books and to Open Library, which return matching covers. Nothing else about you is sent, and you can switch this off in Settings.
  • LibriVox browser. If you browse free public-domain audiobooks, your searches go to archive.org, which hosts the LibriVox catalogue, and the books you choose are downloaded from there.
  • Sharing notes (optional). "Share my notes" packs the notes you wrote and recorded for one book into a file and hands it to whichever app you choose to send it with — a message, an email, a shared folder. Only your own notes are included: never the audiobook itself, never notes other people sent you, and never anything from a book you did not choose. Once you have sent it, that file is in the hands of the person you sent it to.
  • Importing over Wi-Fi. When you open the Wi-Fi import screen, the app briefly runs a small web page on your phone so that a computer on the same Wi-Fi network can send it audio files. It is protected by a one-time code shown on your phone, is reachable only from your own network, and shuts down when you leave the screen.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Music and audio — to find and play the audiobook files on your phone. This is the core function of the app.
  • Microphone — only when you press Record to make a voice note. The app never listens otherwise, and asks for this permission the first time you record, not before.
  • Notifications — for the playback controls on your lock screen and in the notification shade.
  • Network — for Google Drive backup, cover lookups, the LibriVox browser and Wi-Fi import. Playing your books needs no network at all.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, or listening history. Snuggery shows no ads, has no analytics and no crash reporting, and we have no way to identify you.

Purchases

If Snuggery offers a one-time purchase, it is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Children

Snuggery is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
